Withdrawal retains the cached lease through Released, including renewals that leave its gate closed. Its own CallId receipt acts as a permanent transfer tombstone. Reopening applies durable authority with its original request start time, suppresses that transfer's release records, and leaves another drain, Superseded or Retired intact. ## Terminal move and capture restart

Decision 79 uses the proved std hard-link/unlink sequence, without a new dependency or unsafe code. A permanent primitive journey first ran on the worktree's APFS volume: an occupied destination returned AlreadyExists with both files byte-identical, and a process kill between link and unlink retained two names with equal device/inode and unchanged contents (`native_move_link`, one passed, exit 0).

`cargo test -p fleet-sync-writer --offline --lib writer_move_tests` passes three tests (exit 0): the file includes committed WAL data after moving, only durable registration rejection permits a move, guarded readers and occupied destinations refuse before closing the source, path aliases cannot unlink the destination itself, repeats succeed, and a retry never creates a missing source. The first replay exposed a 32-byte WAL header from Turso's verification open; verification now checkpoints and closes on every retry, preserving the strict empty-WAL gate.

`cargo test --manifest-path tests/consumer/Cargo.toml --offline --test native_move_aside -- --nocapture` passes its real process journey (one passed, exit 0). Three registrations are refused over WebSocket; their accepted writes remain as rejected tails. After joining the writer runtime, the mover is killed before link, between link and unlink, or after unlink. Each associated retry succeeds twice, keeps exact integer/blob cells and the original rejected-tail positions, and leaves no source. Source WAL and SHM are absent or empty at every kill boundary. Occupied destinations are refused with both files byte-identical. Strict Clippy for writer/runner/service and the standalone consumer passed after this implementation.

`cargo test --manifest-path tests/consumer/Cargo.toml --offline --test native_capture_restart -- --nocapture` passes one journey (exit 0): the receiver commits a first split part, the socket closes before that answer reaches the sender, and the receiver process is killed while replicated rows remain unpublished. The writer file reopens, the same session reconnects, and the final part commits before another socket close hides its answer. A second receiver restart replays the final receipt as AlreadyApplied. Resending completes the sender's durable acknowledgement without repeating rows or hook effects; subsequent updates and deletes preserve exact cells, composite keys and NULLs.
